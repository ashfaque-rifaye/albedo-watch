# Albedo-Watch — submission text

**Track:** 02 — Clean Air & Climate Resilience

## Brief description (2–3 lines)

Albedo-Watch is live air intelligence on a 3D Earth, built on Gemini and Google Maps Platform: it finds pollution no official monitor sees (NASA fires and citizen photo or voice reports checked against 6,560 reference monitors), traces it to its sources, and forecasts unhealthy air 72 hours ahead for 233 cities, India in state-level depth. It protects the vulnerable, with safe hours and advisories for the schools and hospitals in the smoke and push alerts for citizens, and a Gemini agent, Albedo Copilot, turns one question in any language into an order drafted for the right official, whom a human must approve. States learn from each other through federated learning that shares model weights, never raw data.
